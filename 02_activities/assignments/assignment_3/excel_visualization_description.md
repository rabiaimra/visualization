This line chart illustrates the average of median hourly wages for men and women with full-time employment and age group of 25-54. The dataset consists of 2,736 observations from Ontario's Open Data Catalogue (https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/wages-by-education-level) from 2006 to 2024 . It shows a steady upward trend for both genders, with men’s wages increasing from around $20 in 2006 to over $30 by 2024, and women’s wages rising from slightly above $15 to just over $25. Despite overall growth, a persistent gender wage gap remains across all years, with men consistently earning more than women. The chart includes interactive filters for type of work, education level, age group, and immigrant status, making it a flexible tool for examining wage patterns across subpopulations. This visualization can also support analysis of whether wage growth has kept pace with inflation over time.
