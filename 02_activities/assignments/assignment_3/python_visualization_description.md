Python visualization description: 

This bar chart displays the median hourly wage (in CAD) for men and women aged 25–54, segmented by education level and employment type (all employees, full-time, and part-time). The sample size used for this chart includes 5,472 observations from Ontario's Open Data Catalogue (https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/wages-by-education-level), and a colorblind-friendly palette in the chart ensures accessibility. For all employees, across all education levels, men consistently earn more than women. The wage gap is even more pronounced among higher education levels in full-time employment. In contrast, for part-time work, women earn slightly more or close to men, although overall wages are lower for both groups. Notably, the graph also shows that higher levels of education are associated with higher wages overall, regardless of gender.
